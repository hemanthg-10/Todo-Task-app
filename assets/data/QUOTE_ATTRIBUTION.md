# Quote collection attribution

The 500 English motivational quote records shipped in `quotes.json` were selected from the quote dataset published by [MindArchive](https://github.com/GGNadirashvili/MindArchive), which identifies Wikiquote as the quote source.

The Wikiquote material is available under the [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International license (CC BY-SA 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). The app's selection and category labels are adapted from that dataset; quote text and author attributions are retained. The per-quote author is shown in the Home quote carousel.
